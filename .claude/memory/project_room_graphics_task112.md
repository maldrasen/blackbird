Task 112 (room graphics) was developed on the `112-room-graphics` git branch and CLOSED OUT 2026-07-17 — the oblique-projection room/wall/door rendering below is the shipped state. Follow-on work was split into separate tasks the user is creating: door visual tweaks, dungeon themes, and procedural wall/floor patterns. (Until the branch merges, `main` still renders rooms as HTML `roomBox` divs on gridSize 32 with no GeometryHelper.)

- Decision: each room and door is its own `<svg>` element (not one floor-wide SVG) — keeps z-index depth layering, room-local coordinates, and CSS/room-level effects.
- Walls are ORTHOGONAL (2026-07-17 plan update): only north walls (fully visible) and west walls (partially visible) are drawn; the earlier per-room fake-perspective idea was dropped (unworkable for 1-tile corridors). Doors get painted directly on the N/W walls.
- Door model flipped to match: `{position, direction:'N'|'W', from, to}` — position tile owns the door on its N/W wall and belongs to `from`; `to` is across the wall. Flipped in door-finder, floor-factory-support buildDoor, nested-room, floor view. Saves from before the flip have stale S/E doors.
- Rooms render as two polygons: `.footprint` (full grid shape, bg #111, no stroke — alone it's the hidden/unrevealed state, occluding nested rooms) plus `.walls` (outline inset 8px, fill:none stroke line, hidden while unrevealed; current room adds a fill highlight). GeometryHelper (application/helpers/geometry-helper.js) provides `traceOutline(footprint)` and `insetOutline(vertices, inset)` — headless-testable (specs build a Room and pass room.getFootprint()).
- Doors are clickable, hidden until either side is revealed; `?` pads still render for unexplored navigation.
- A containing room draws `.nested-wall` outlines (exterior faces) for deeper rooms of its feature, outset by wallInset — matching the breathing room between adjacent rooms AND landing the exterior face bands exactly on the neighboring tiles' interior band positions, so box-side doors align with zero special casing; dungeon colors live in variables.scss `=== Dungeon ===` section; console command `reveal` (DungeonCommands.revealAll) reveals the whole floor for visual checks.
- `DungeonRoomView.build(floor, room)` builds the room SVG; rendering must stay out of the Room model.
- gridSize is 64 with zoom scaleSteps [0.25, 0.375, 0.5, 0.75, 1], default 0.5 (visually identical to the old 32px/0.5–2 setup, double drawing resolution). DungeonViewport.reset() must apply scale(0.5) explicitly since a rebuilt #dungeonFloor has no transform.
- Walls are drawn in oblique projection with OCCLUSION-CORRECT corners (settled 2026-07-17 after failed attempts at forcing diagonals everywhere): `.walls` is the plain inset-8 outline; each visible N/W face's base line = its ceiling segment + v where v = (westWallDepth, northWallDepth) = (10,20). Face-end rule (DungeonRoomView.wallFaceLines): at a CONVEX corner the end edge hides behind the side wall → trim the base to the per-direction-inset T-point on the side wall line, no cap; at a CONCAVE corner (inside corners of crosses/Ls) the end edge stands exposed → base keeps its projected end (wallLine[k]+v) and a `.wall-corner` diagonal connects the ceiling corner to it. Both-faced (NW-type) corners miter and get their diagonal from cornerLines. GeometryHelper has outlineRuns (index chains) + vertexIsConvex. Nested exteriors keep shiftOutline splits — convex solids, all silhouette edges.
- Doors: a single solid parallelogram (`.slab`, fill $fg-dungeon-wall) on the from-room's wall face — doorLength 20 wide, base on the wall base, top stopping doorLintel (6) short of the wall line, 45° lean, whole shape centered on the tile. The slab's TOP edge is centered on the tile (the base trails the lean) — the top edge is the visually dominant part, so this is what reads as centered; base-centering and bounding-box-centering both looked left-shifted. Width capped by the corner-tile worst case at gridSize − 2·wallInset − 2·lean = 24 so the slab never needs occlusion (base stays between the NW miter and the far side wall even on 1-tile rooms; base's far corner runs within 2px of the side wall at the cap). Both wall depths are 20 (45° corners, equal-size W doors; wallInset 6). A door whose from-room is unexplored "hangs" in the blank — intentionally (a known door shouldn't shift on reveal) — and carries a tile-width `.frame` (wall + floor line at the exact positions the room's real lines will occupy); revealing the from-room adds `.anchored` (build + updateLocation), hiding the frame seamlessly.
- Doors are CLOSED slabs (DungeonDoorView): a `.mouth` rect (bg fill+stroke for 1px bleed) erases lines crossing the passage, then redraw: flat passage side lines, cut-edge diagonals slanting up-left (-v) from the wall base, ceiling+base segments carried across, and a `.slab` door-top line at 80% wall height (slabHeight const; lintel is only 2px at current metrics — tune if too thin). Free line ends use stroke-linecap: square to avoid corner notches.
