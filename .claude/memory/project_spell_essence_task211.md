Tasks 211 and 219 shipped 2026-09-12 (219 was briefly numbered 218, which collided with the completed poised task; renumbered 2026-09-13): `EssenceSystem` prices `monster-cast-spell` entries and natural attacks (beast-bite, leap-claw, punch, venomous-bite) instead of hand-set `essence` values. Post-review simplification pass 2026-09-13 on branch `211-calculate-essence-for-spells`.

- Core: `EssenceSystem.effectsEssenceBreakdown({ effects, targets, period, spike })`, spike defaults to `EffectMath.averageDamage(effects)`. Damage = spike * (spike * targets / periodSec) * 0.045 (spike-weighted so burst > steady). Status = type.getEssence() * targets * min(1, duration/period), plus a DoT term (expected lifetime damage priced as one burst), both discounted by `EffectMath.landChance(strength)`. `periodBetweenUses(useTime, cooldown)` = max of the two.
- Spells: `spellEssenceBreakdown(entry)`, period = castTime + BattleConstants.spellReleaseTime vs cooldown; targets single 1 / enemyFormation 3 / small area 2 / self+ally 0. Natural attacks: `attackEssenceBreakdown({ low, high, speed, cooldown, effects })` delegates to the effects breakdown with spike = (low+high)/2; `NaturalAttackAbility` records with no `essence` calculate from the entry, and may declare `getEffects(entry)` (venomous-bite → Effect.poison). Since 2026-09-13 the factory applies `getEffects` on hit through `EffectSystem.applyStatus` (exported, resist roll + addStatus) and calls `messageForEntity(target, results)` for the flavor line, so battle and essence share one declaration.
- Knobs at the top of `application/characters/essence-system.js` (damageEssenceScale 0.045; formationTargets 3; smallAreaTargets 2). `StatusEffectType` records carry `essence` = threat of one enemy permanently under the effect (blind 15, stun 40, rest 0 and reported as "unpriced"; deliberately lenient, no throw).
- Hook: `EssenceSystem.abilityEntryBreakdown(base, key)` is the single place deciding hand-set (`{ total, handSet:true }`, entry `essence` wins, nightgaunt keeps 50 on purpose) vs `Ability.lookup(code).getEssenceBreakdown(entry)` with cooldown resolved via `BaseMonster.getAbilityCooldown(key)`. `getAbilityMap()`/`getAbilityCooldown()` now live on the BaseMonster record; the Monster wrapper delegates (default `defend` key has no cooldown). There is no `getEssence()`/`spellEssence()` any more, read `.total`.
- Still hand-set by design: basic-attack 10, hide 10, sneak-attack 25, basic-defend 5, dick-punch 75. monster-use-article is unpriced (0).
- `EffectMath` (application/effect-math.js): averageDamage / landChance / statusDurationSeconds (fixedTime, turnCount, untilResisted via expectedTicks; throws on special/until-cured/fixed-count) / interval, shared with `ArticleAppraiser`.
- `node bin/reports/ability-essence-report.js` prints status effect essence (flagging unpriced), spell × power level, every monster ability entry with kind (attack/spell/hand-set/unpriced), resolved cooldown and terms, and per-monster sums.

**Superseded on branch `221-refactor-abilities` (2026-09-15, shipped as 5 reviewed steps):** pricing lives in `AbilityAppraiser` (application/battle-abilities/ability-appraiser.js). `Ability.getEssence(attributes)` is lazy: hand-set value wins, else the factory's appraise closure runs with the attributes of the monster using it, else 0; no `run()` at init any more. `attackEssence(options, attributes)` prices a natural attack as spike = midpoint of [low,high] / 100 * strength (damage-roll.js: the range is a % of strength, not raw damage), throws without attributes; cast-spell memoizes in its own closure. `spikeWeight` = dps * spike^0.35 * 5. `EssenceSystem.monsterEssenceValue` = round((sum of ability essence at Attributes(id) + bonus) * healthFactor * (1/speedFactor for beasts) * essenceScale 10); no attribute term, no squeeze. Weapon-only monsters (armed kobolds, vermen-ragpicker) read 0 by design until task 220; `EncounterBuilder.pricedMonsters` skips them. `data/essence-data.js` regenerated at scale 10 (level-1 beasts ~30, daggermaw avg ~1030 at level 5 which the builder can't afford until ~level 9; kobold 0.2 health factor makes kobolds tiny). Base-monster nominal attributes path deferred to 220. Loot spec bonus is coupled to the scale (125 now). Known loose end: `tickDamageEssence` overcounts a DoT whose duration exceeds the cast period.

**Why:** the user decided (2026-09-12) that essence is intrinsic, the concentrated magic a monster contains: attributes, natural abilities, and spells count, equipment and carried items do not. Essence also drives the encounter builder and loot sizing, which is the wrong meaning for those; task 220 splits out a "challenge rating" (the word "threat" is taken by battle targeting) that adds weapons, armor, and consumables.

**How to apply:** don't price equipment or items into essence. Tune through the knobs and status `essence` values, then rerun `node bin/compile-essence-data.js`. That compile is deterministic, but `data/essence-data.js` drifts silently whenever monster data changes without a rerun, so regenerate it after any monster/ability data change.
