
# Episode Notes

### Writing Strategy
Writing is hard, and for whatever reason episode text is a lot harder for me than adding other types of content. I
need to have a strategy for writing episode text so that it doesn't feel like hitting a brick wall whenever I need to
work on one. 

- First, when adding a new episode, I need to remember that the episode text is really just a rough draft. I don't
  need the text to be perfect. Better to get something rough down than to leave it as something to do later.
- Getting a production ready version on the first pass of an episode might be a huge waste of time, as subtle details,
  or perhaps even the entire episode, will need to change as more episodes are added.
- Different replacement tokens and state values that I'll need to consult may also be added after the initial version
  of the episode was written.
- I should keep a log in a comment in each episode file, so I know what state an episode text is in. Is it still a 
  rough draft? Is it in a fairly solid state? What important facts are being established by this event?
