The setting doesn't have true gods. The dungeon though has created its own god like and mythological figures. It creates the statues and reliefs and such that are found throughout the dungeon. In a sense, the dungeon is communicating though symbolism, though never in a very clear manner. Delvers see characters depicted in statues, but they're never named. The only information they have about these characters are half made up by the scholars that study the dungeon. The scholars a bit like archeologists, putting together an entire mythology from the ruins and few scraps of art from a long dead civilization.
