I didn't want to put the whole "how dungeon tripe got it's name" story in the item description. We should add an episode where one of the dungeon monsters explains it though. First, Rock Tripe is a famine food, eaten in North America, which is where I got the idea for a dungeon tripe. Some lichens have a honeycomb like texture (disc-like reproductive structures called apothecia) which have an appearance similar to reticulum beef tripe, the folds in the stomach chambers.

Just a good opportunity for a flavor episode, something to talk with your party members about.
