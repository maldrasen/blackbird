Abilities like the bite attack also need to have adjustable damage and essence properties that cab be accessed when the ability is used and in the enlighten system. We already have a pattern for this. When we apply poison damage we can get the damage from the base monster's ability map. We just need to do the same when we read the damage and essence to award.

My thinking here is that weapons have damage ranges based on the weapon types. This gives equipment variety and makes a hand axe different from a war axe. Beast bite attacks don't need variety in this way, but they do need a range of damage values so that a kobold bite can be a different class than a dragon bite.

Because the damage ranges can vary wildly between abilities we need to be able to adjust the essence awarded in each ability, in order to weigh a deadly poison differently than a weak one.
