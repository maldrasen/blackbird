Actions you take in battle should adjust your party member's feelings. First, if a party member is knocked out during a fight they should lose a large chunk of respect for you. Winning any fight should also earn respect. A difficult fight (however that is determined) should earn more respect than an easy fight.

### Defend
We need to give tank characters a defend ability that lets them take hits for other characters. Taking a hit could also generate a large amount of affection and respect, and it's a useful ability to have. This should probably be its own task though.

### Future Work (Once we have spells)
Healing a character should earn respect and affection, but only when a character is injured. A character at 2/3 health should get a small boost and a large one at 1/3. Because each feelings component is associated with a single entity, it's the feelings for the healer that are being changed, so if the player wants the healing affection, then they need to be the healer. Otherwise you party members will just love their healer, which is a good thing as well of course. Other future spells will have similar effects. Buff spells could also earn a little respect.
