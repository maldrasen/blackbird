One useful event type to have would be finding trainers that could give you points in one skill or another, especially when you only have a few points (or maybe no points) in a skill.

> This episode would utilize the new Person and Conversation models. The people you could run into who would teach you various skills would be reoccurring NPC. They would need their own dialog trees. At first they could just be giving you some pointers, giving you a few skill points. Once you're skilled though they could also start teaching you abilities or spells. These might require you to do some work for them though. Quest type stuff, raising their respect for you, before they teach you anything good.
