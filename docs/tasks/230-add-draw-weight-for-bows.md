Bow damage should actually scale with strength, but an archer's strength should effect what kind of bows they're able to use. Bows should have a draw weight, which is the minimum strength necessary to fully draw a bow. A bow can't be equipped if the character doesn't have enough strength to use it. Actually this could be applied to other weapons as well, mauls for instance should have a minimum strength as well. Bow damage though doesn't scale with strength. It should be flat. A stronger character can't really fire an arrow harder, they're limited by what the bow can do. 

Crossbows won't have a strength requirement, and they'll still hit as hard as a high draw strength bow. (Crossbow damage still shouldn't scale with strength.) The tradeoff is they take longer to fire because they need to be cranked to reload.
