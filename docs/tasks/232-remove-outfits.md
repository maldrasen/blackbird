When designing the equipment, I had thought it would be a good idea to separate armor and clothing, including "under" slots for clothing that would be worn under armor and outfit type items. I'm thinking now that all that really just adds unneeded complexity. We can still add shirts and robes and things and have them take same equipment slots that the armor would. They'll just be a type of armor, and could also be enchanted.

I'm still going to be adding a accessory item type, for rings and jewelry and body piercings, but that's a separate issue entirely.
