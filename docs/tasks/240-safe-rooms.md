We should add safe rooms to the dungeon, probably as a new feature type. Primarily, the safe rooms are there to provide an area to rest, allowing the party to heal and restore mana. Camping in the dungeon will eventually be a whole feature, giving you a chance to interact with your party members. You should be able to have conversations with party members, and take time to train them.
