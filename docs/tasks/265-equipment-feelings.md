We've added an EquipmentTaste module, used by the character equipper when first choosing the equipment for a character. We need to take these taste preferences into consideration when changing a character's equipment. A character could refuse to wear something they hate, and after an initial refusal, the player could order them to wear something.

This will tie into the feelings systems. Wearing equipment they really like should increase a character's positive feelings, but being forced to wear equipment they hate, or being stripped naked should generate negative feelings. This will also tie into the anima component as well, and being stripped naked or forced to wear something they don't like would build shame anima.

This isn't that important now, but will be once we start adding more sex toys as items. Most characters should have opinions about being equipped with an anal plug or nipple clamps.
