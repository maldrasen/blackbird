When we enter a new room in the dungeon we should roll a scouting check. If a room has a trap or treasure it may not be obvious. A failed scouting check will just trip the trap as soon as the party enters the room. A successful scouting check will be needed to find a hidden treasure, otherwise the room looks empty. Room contents will need some kind of hidden difficulty level to roll against. Some traps are easier to spot than others. 

The scouting roll should always be rolled by a single party member. It makes sense to make this position based. Whoever is in the front center position (or nearest to it) should be the character making that roll. Scouting is intelligence based, which gives an incentive to have at least one smart person in the front row.
