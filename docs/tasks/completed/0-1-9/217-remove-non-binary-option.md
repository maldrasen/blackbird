I wanted to add a non-binary gender option, but it's just not working out. The problem is essentially a language one. They/them is difficult to work with within the weaver template because of subject verb agreement. I tried ze/zem and don't like the way it sounds. Also, because I use pronouns in the battle text, it doesn't really make sense that you'd know a monster's pronouns and such as soon as they attack you. 

I think I'll just remove the option from generation. Perhaps I'll go back and allow pronouns to be assigned to specific characters, and let the explicitly set pronouns overwrite the the defaults. For right now though, there are just too many problems to have to work around.
